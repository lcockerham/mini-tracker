# AD&D 2e catalog enrichment exceptions

Started: 2026-09-04

Baseline inventory: 309 AD&D 2e records. Seven records already contained a
product URL when the full-catalog pass began; 302 records were pending.

Pass result: all 309 records were reviewed. Of those, 222 are complete with a
verified URL, ownership state, and local cover; 87 remain incomplete and are
listed below (59 metadata conflicts, 27 titles with no matching listing, and
one unavailable cover). One additional completed record is noted because it
duplicates another collection entry. The signed-in check marked 31 records as
owned digitally.

**2026-09-07 update:** Cross-referenced every "explicitly classified as Basic"
metadata conflict against Shannon Appelcline's *Designers & Dragons: Origins*,
Vol. 3 and Vol. 4 — a publishing history of TSR/D&D whose tables of contents
happen to enumerate the entire Basic/Expert/Companion/Master/Immortals (BECMI)
and Mystara product line (GAZ-series Gazetteers, HW-series Hollow World,
DDA-series Dawn of the Emperors, PC-series Creature Crucible, the Rules
Cyclopedia, Poor Wizard's Almanacs, Wrath of the Immortals, etc.). Every one of
the 56 records below appears in that lineup, independently confirming DMsGuild
was right and the collection's AD&D 2e classification is wrong — these should
all be moved to D&D Basic, the same category the earlier system audit already
used for the AD&D 1e/BECMI split (see docs/adnd-1e-catalog-exceptions.md). Deep
Dwarven Delve (251) was separately confirmed as AD&D 1e (not 2e or Basic) via
Vol. 1. The remaining "No listing" and core-rulebook rows were not mentioned in
the books and are left unchanged.

**2026-09-07 applied:** After backing up `mini_tracker.db`, all 56 confirmed
records were reclassified (55 to D&D Basic, Deep Dwarven Delve to AD&D 1e). 52
of them had a cited DMsGuild product ID; their cover images were downloaded
from DMsGuild's CDN into `app/static/images/books/` and their DMsGuild product
URLs were saved to `drivethrurpg_url`. The remaining four reclassified records
(225, 227, 438, 442) have no known DMsGuild listing, so no cover/URL was set
for them.
